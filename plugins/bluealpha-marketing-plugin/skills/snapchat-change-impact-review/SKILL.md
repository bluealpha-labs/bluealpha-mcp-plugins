---
name: snapchat-change-impact-review
description: "Find out whether recent edits to a Snapchat Ads account helped or hurt. Reads the change history of the campaigns, ad squads, ads and creatives that spent, then compares the 7 days before and after each budget, bid, bid strategy, targeting, goal, status or creative change on the metric each ad squad optimizes for, against an unchanged control where one exists. Flags changes too recent to judge and changes that overlap. Can revert one budget or bid change on request. Use when the user says 'what changed in my Snapchat account', 'Snapchat change impact', 'did my Snapchat budget change work', 'why did Snapchat performance drop', 'Snapchat change history', or wants to know which Snapchat edits moved results."
